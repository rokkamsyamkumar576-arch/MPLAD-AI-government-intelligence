Thousands of projects. Manual eyes can't cover all of them.

MPLADS gives Members of Parliament funds to recommend local development projects — spread across every state, thousands of works, and years of financial and progress data.

Monitoring all of it by hand makes it easy to miss the patterns that matter: spending that outpaces construction, projects quietly running late, work that duplicates something nearby, or costs that drift upward without explanation.

By the time these issues surface manually, the money is often already spent.

Unusual spending patterns

Hard to notice when payments don't match construction pace.

Cost overruns

Revised estimates can slip past without a consistent check.

Delays

Timeline slippage across states is difficult to compare at scale.

Duplicate works

Similar projects nearby can go unnoticed without geographic analysis.

Financial vs. physical mismatch

Money spent and work completed don't always tell the same story.

MPLAD-AI reads the data so a person can decide.

One pipeline turns raw project records into a prioritized, explainable list — so inspection teams know where to look first.

MPLAD-AI is a decision-support system. It does not declare fraud. It flags potentially risky patterns and routes them for human verification — the final call always stays with the authority.

How it works

From raw records to a verified outcome, in eight steps.

01

Data Collection

Pull project, financial, and progress records from MPLADS / eSAKSHI sources.

02

Data Cleaning

Normalize, de-duplicate, and validate fields across states and years.

03

AI/ML Analysis

Run financial, spatial, and text-similarity models across the cleaned dataset.

04

Anomaly Detection

Surface outliers in spend, payments, and progress reporting.

05

Risk Scoring

Combine signals into a single 0–100 score with a risk category.

06

Explainable AI

Use SHAP / LIME to show exactly which signals drove the score.

07

Alerts

Route high and critical-risk projects to the right inspection queue.

08

Authority Verification

A human reviewer confirms, dismisses, or escalates each flagged case.

Key AI features

Twelve capabilities working together across detection, explanation, and access.

Anomaly Detection

Isolation Forest flags irregular spend and project patterns early.

Cost Overrun Detection

Tracks estimate revisions against sector and district norms.

Progress Mismatch

Compares financial progress against reported physical completion.

Duplicate Work Detection

Sentence-similarity models catch overlapping or repeated works.

Contractor Risk Analysis

Surfaces patterns across implementing agencies over time.

Delay Prediction

Random Forest / XGBoost estimate the likelihood of timeline slippage.

Explainable AI

SHAP and LIME translate model output into plain-language reasons.

Interactive Map

Geographic view of projects, clusters, and proximity risk.

Citizen Feedback

A channel for on-ground observations to feed into review.

Automated Reports

One-click risk investigation reports, filterable by state, year, or MP.

Voice Assistant

Ask project or portfolio questions in natural language.

Real-Time Alerts

New high-risk findings are routed the moment they're detected.
